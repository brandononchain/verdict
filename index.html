                    Search the web. See the reasoning.

                    A space for discovery.

                    1. SearchZearch searches the web and reads the pages it finds.
                    2. SelectJev, the evidence checker, picks the passages that matter.
                    3. CheckEach drafted paragraph is checked against its sources.

                    Jev judges whether the passages can answer your question. A writing model then drafts short, cited paragraphs, and Jev checks each one against its sources before you see it.

                    If a draft fails that check, Zearch shows the exact passage Jev selected instead. Open the original sources for context. A citation points to evidence; it does not guarantee the answer is right.
