                  A space for discovery.

                  Zearch searches the web and reads the pages it finds. Jev, Zearch’s evidence checker, picks the passages that matter and judges whether they can answer your question. A writing model then drafts short, cited paragraphs, and Jev checks each one against its sources before you see it.

                  If a draft fails that check, Zearch shows the exact passage Jev selected instead. Open the original sources for context. A citation points to evidence; it does not guarantee the answer is right.
