    Search the web. See the reasoning.

    Zearch is an open-source search interface powered by JEV (TypeSafe AI). It gathers web evidence, sends a structured state to the model, and shows its typed answers and confidence gate alongside the sources.

    Use it for open-ended research or questions with a finite decision space. The engine can return act, review, or abstain; your team remains responsible for what happens next.

    Sources
    Search results are packed and deduplicated across the web, Wikipedia, instant answers, and a small seed corpus.
    Answers
    Playbooks define a finite set of typed questions. Confidence scores and thresholds remain visible.
    Engine
    Connect a server-side TypeSafe key for live answers, or use the clearly labeled offline mock mode.
    Limits
    Model calibration is vendor-claimed. Verify source quality and tune confidence thresholds for your use case.
